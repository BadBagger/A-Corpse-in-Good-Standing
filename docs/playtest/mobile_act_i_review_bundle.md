# Mobile Act I Review Bundle

Use this when you are on a phone and cannot run the Godot build. This is not a replacement for playing Act I, but it is enough to judge visual direction, Corvin side-action readability, and whether the project should continue into Act II greybox after the real Act I review.

## Quick Verdict

Reply with one of these:

- `keep going`
- `fix Corvin first`
- `fix rooms first`
- `fix duel/UI first`
- `not this visual direction`

## 1. Project Look Target

This is the broad mood/UI/composition target. It is reference only, not production art.

![Project visual target](../art/review/project_visual_target_mockup_v1.png)

Check:

- Does this look like the right game?
- Is the harbor ribcage idea strong enough?
- Does amber/green lighting feel right?
- Is the bottom UI density acceptable?

## 2. Corvin Side Actions

This sheet is the important one for your last note: he should read side-facing and doing distinct actions, not just standing forward.

![Corvin side actions](../art/review/corvin_side_actions_contact_sheet.png)

Check:

- Do idle, talk, use, and wet read as different enough?
- Does Corvin face sideways enough for adventure-game play?
- Is the wet/dripping gag visible enough?

## 3. Act I Look Target

This is the older Act I look target that the room art pipeline was already using.

![Act I harbor look target](../art/reference/look_targets/act_i_harbor_look_target_v1.png)

Check:

- Should the final game lean closer to this rough/painterly target or the newer mockup?
- Does the side-on walk band feel clear?
